React Native · Arcade Game

Orbs Soul

Orbs Soul is a fast-paced arcade game built in React Native — a blend of flappy-bird-style flight and an endless road-runner. You pilot a glowing orb, jumping and flying to stay safe from obstacles while the level scrolls faster and faster. Along the way you collect power orbs, each holding a unique ability — Ascend to fly above the road, Inferno to burn through hazards, Chrono to slow time, Aegis to shield a hit and Bolt for a speed burst. Build a collection of 30 orbs and evolve your own play style.

  • Flappy-style flight + endless runner
  • Collectible power orbs
  • 5 unique abilities
  • 30-orb collection to evolve
Flutter · Budget & Expense Tracker

Grocery Calculator

Grocery Calculator is a personal budgeting app that makes it easy to track daily grocery and everyday expenses against a monthly budget. Add each item with its price, see how much you have left this month at a glance, and dig into analytics that compare spending month-over-month and celebrate your savings. Set recurring reminders for budget tasks, invite family or others into a shared workspace to manage money together, pick your currency, and export everything to CSV.

  • Daily expense & budget tracking
  • Spending & savings analytics
  • Recurring reminders
  • Shared family workspace
  • Multi-currency & CSV export
